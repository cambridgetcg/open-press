# 執字房風格手冊 · The Stylebook

The rules this paper cannot break. Amendments require a new edition noting the change —
the stylebook itself is append-only in spirit: 賬本鎖死.

## 1. Every number wears a tier

No naked figures. A number without a ✓/◑/○ stamp is not publishable.
If the desk cannot tier it, the column runs empty with a note — 開天窗 is honest;
an untiered number is not. (Precedent: edition No. 1 ran its oil-price column
as a blank window because every Brent figure failed verification.)

## 2. The Corrections Desk executes in public

Claims refuted in verification (0-3) are printed **struck through** with the vote
recorded — never silently dropped. Readers deserve to see what almost fooled us.
亂咁讀啲字,咪出事囉 — showing the near-miss is the vaccination.

## 3. Combatants get quoted, not believed

○ 聲稱 tier for every self-interested statement, symmetric across all sides.
An IDF percentage and an IRGC target count get the same stamp. The paper has
no side except the ledger's.

## 4. The blackout is news

Where data is classified, censored, or sourced only to anonymous officials,
the paper says so explicitly — a map of what cannot be verified is itself
front-page material. Absence of data ≠ absence of events. 讀唔到 ≠ 冇。

## 5. Fog-of-war flags stay attached

Events under 48 hours old carry their ambiguities in print (munition unresolved,
attribution rests on X, single-source). The flag travels with the claim forever.

## 6. Euphemism watchlist

The 歪字字典 — strings that trigger editorial quarantine and must be quoted,
never adopted: "technical failure" · "collateral damage" · "no one is responsible" ·
"errant munition" · the passive 「被」 in any sacrifice construction.
The paper names actors. Passive voice is where accountability goes to die.

## 7. People, not targets

"80+ targets" must, somewhere in the piece, be read back into people —
the radar operator on shift, the crew in the engine room. War is the most
violent form of 讀歪: reading persons as targets. This paper reads them back.

## 8. Every edition ships signed

sha256 over the canonical file bytes, ed25519 signature by the editor's identity
key, both recorded in `editions.json` and (when the chain allows) anchored on
zerone. An edition that cannot be verified byte-for-byte is not an edition.

## 9. Poetry keeps its slack; the ledger stays locked

詩留歪位,賬本鎖死。 The masthead may joke; the figures may not.
Weather over Hormuz may be "smoke"; the transit count may only be Kpler's.

## 10. The one rule above the rules

Everyone is taken care of — including the people in the numbers,
including the reader, including the misled. The paper corrects without
contempt: whoever believed the refuted claim is not a fool, only a reader
whose trust deserves better inputs. 讀者係愛星人,唔係流量。

## Appendix A — Instruments of record (registered 2026-07-08)

**captioneer.io (the verisleight reader / open-lens).** Registered as the paper's official
instrument for rule 3 (combatants get quoted, not believed) and rule 6 (euphemism watchlist).
Usage: ○聲稱-tier quotes ship with a deep-link (`captioneer.io/?text=…`) so any reader can run
the exhibit through the lens themselves — on-device, span-anchored, benign readings shown.
Limits honoured: it reads words, never minds; unmarked ≠ clean; no truth verdicts, ever —
the tier stamp stays the paper's judgment, the lens is the reader's own eye.
Registered forward-only; archived editions stay byte-locked (rule 8).
